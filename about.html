Freedom Racing Enterprises

ABOUT THE TEAM

Freedom Racing Enterprises (FRE) was founded in 2024. Based in Mooresville, North Carolina, the team fields the No. 76 and No. 67 Chevrolet Silverados in the NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series.

Veteran NASCAR crew chief and broadcaster Jeff Hammond serves as General Manager. The team races with a focus on performance, partnership, and putting brands in front of passionate NASCAR fans.

The #76

The No. 76 Chevrolet Silverado is the team's full-time entry. Primary sponsors have included Freedom Warranty, Nor-Cal Equipment Rentals, and others. The truck competes across the full 25-race schedule.

The #67

The No. 67 Chevrolet Silverado gives Freedom Racing Enterprises a second entry. It provides additional opportunities for drivers and sponsors while expanding the team's presence on the Truck Series circuit.

Team Info

  • Series: NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series
  • Trucks: #76 & #67 Chevrolet Silverados
  • Base: Mooresville, North Carolina
  • Founded: 2024
  • General Manager: Jeff Hammond
