Freedom Racing Enterprises

ABOUT THE TEAM

Freedom Racing Enterprises (FRE) was founded in 2024 by Chris Miller and Spencer Boyd. Based in Mooresville, North Carolina, the team fields the No. 76 Chevrolet Silverado full-time in the NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series.

Veteran NASCAR crew chief and broadcaster Jeff Hammond serves as General Manager. The team races with a focus on performance, partnership, and putting brands in front of passionate NASCAR fans.

Spencer Boyd – Driver & Co-Owner

Spencer Boyd is a professional NASCAR driver and co-owner of Freedom Racing Enterprises. Known for his underdog spirit and memorable moments (including a Talladega win earlier in his career), Boyd pilots the #76 for the majority of the season while also developing other drivers in the truck.

The #76

The No. 76 Chevrolet Silverado represents Freedom Racing Enterprises on the national stage. Primary sponsors have included Freedom Warranty, Nor-Cal Equipment Rentals, and others. The team continues to build competitive identity race by race.
